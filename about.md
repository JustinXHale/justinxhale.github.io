# About Me

Hi, I'm Justin X. Hale, a Senior Product Designer at Red Hat. I work on developer tools and AI platform experiences: making logs, events, and configuration failures understandable so engineers can act. I ship coded prototypes when that is the fastest path to clarity.

As design moved from Figma to coded prototypes, I started building tools the org did not have yet. I originated [design-comments]({{ '/portfolio/design-comments/design-comments/' | relative_url }}) (PatternFly maintains it now) for structured feedback on running UI. Outside Red Hat I publish a [suite of real products]({{ '/portfolio/product-suite/product-suite/' | relative_url }}) for sports officials and event crews, including a LiveKit-powered voice app ([Tō3]({{ '/portfolio/to3/to3/' | relative_url }})).

When I'm not designing, I'm often on the rugby pitch as a referee. Rugby taught me to make clear calls under pressure and to respect the team around me. Both show up in how I design complex workflows.

I'm a husband and dad. Family keeps me grounded.

If I had to sum it up: curious problem-solver, systems thinker, and collaborator who cares about what ships, not just what gets mocked up.

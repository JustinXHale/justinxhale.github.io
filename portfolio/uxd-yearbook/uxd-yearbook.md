## The problem

Red Hat UXD adopted AI-assisted prototyping (Cursor, PatternFly MCP, GitLab workflows) faster than the org had a safe, shared on-ramp. Designers needed a structured path, not ad hoc Slack threads, to learn vibe coding without breaking production repos.

## What I built

I founded **UXD Yearbook**: a living, coded directory concept where designers complete a **6-stage journey** by merging their own bio card:

1. Authentication & environment (Cursor license, PatternFly MCP)
2. Forking & branching in GitLab
3. Vibe coding: AI interview generates member folder and JSON
4. System alignment: verify card in local gallery
5. Push to personal branch
6. Merge request: peer review; join the team gallery

The project doubles as training infrastructure and a team directory. Designers learn the toolchain by shipping something real.

- Program **designed and initiated**, but the goal of org-wide participation did not fully materialize

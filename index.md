# Welcome to Justin X. Hale's Portfolio

I'm a Senior Product Designer at Red Hat. I design developer tools and AI platform surfaces: turning opaque runtime failures (logs, events, bad configs) into interfaces engineers can scan and act on. I build coded prototypes when that is the fastest path to clarity, and outside work I ship real products on LiveKit, Flutter, and the web.

At Red Hat I originated [design-comments]({{ '/portfolio/design-comments/design-comments/' | relative_url }}) (now maintained by PatternFly) for in-context feedback on coded prototypes. Outside work I design, build, and publish a [suite of live apps]({{ '/portfolio/product-suite/product-suite/' | relative_url }}) for sports officials and event crews.

## Selected work

### [Tō3](./portfolio/to3/to3.md)

LiveKit-powered real-time voice for event crews. Server-issued JWT grants (publish vs. subscribe), connection-test vs. live rooms, and dense live-room UI built for field pressure. Flutter app with WebRTC and audio-session debugging.

### [design-comments](./portfolio/design-comments/design-comments.md)

As design moved off Figma onto coded prototypes, UXD lost in-context commenting. I architected design-comments (pins, GitHub Issues backend, AI summarize). I originated it; PatternFly now maintains it.

### [Published product suite](./portfolio/product-suite/product-suite.md)

Schedule → travel → live voice → review: MatchReadyTX, Match Calendar, Tō3, RefLog, RefereeIQ, and more. Live PWAs and store apps with real cross-product sync and field testing.

### [vLLM CLI UX](./portfolio/vllm-cli-ux/vllm-cli-ux.md)

Redesigned help for 200+ configuration options; merged upstream after an RFC with maintainers.

### [Red Hat Packages Catalog](./portfolio/ui-packages-redhat/ui-packages-redhat.md)

Trusted Libraries and AI Components lived in separate experiences. I unified them into one faceted catalog as a React/TypeScript PatternFly 6 prototype in a week (not yet the live production site).
